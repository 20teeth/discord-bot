# Code of Conduct

20t is a nonprofit club and this project should feel welcoming, respectful, and collaborative.

## Our Standards

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent and give helpful feedback.
- Keep discussions inclusive and professional.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private information without consent.
- Disruptive or hostile behavior that harms collaboration.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness a problem, please contact a club moderator.

We appreciate everyone who helps keep this community healthy.
